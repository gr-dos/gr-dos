Topic: DIR
Summary: Lists the files and subdirectories of a directory
Origin: MS-DOS 4.00
Group: internal

Syntax
  DIR [drive:][path][filename] [/P] [/W]

  filename     The files to list; wildcards (* and ?) may be used. All
               files (*.*) if left out.
  /P           Stops after each screenful; press a key to go on.
  /W           Lists the names only, five to a line.

Examples
  DIR
    Lists the current directory.
  DIR A:\*.TXT /W
    Lists the .TXT files in the root of A:, by name.

Notes
  DIR shows each file's size and the date and time it was last changed,
  and ends with the number of files and the bytes free on the disk.
  Hidden and system files, such as IO.SYS, are not listed. DIR has no
  switch to sort: use DIR | SORT.
