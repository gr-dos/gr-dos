Topic: TIME
Summary: Shows or sets the time
Origin: MS-DOS 4.00
Group: internal

Syntax
  TIME [hh[:mm[:ss[.xx]]]] [A | P]

  hh           Hours, 0 to 23; mm minutes, ss seconds, xx hundredths.
               Parts left out are set to 0.
  A | P        AM or PM, for a 12-hour time (AM, PM, A or P).

Examples
  TIME
    Shows the time and asks for a new one; press Enter to keep it.
  TIME 13:45
    Sets the time to 1:45 in the afternoon.
  TIME 1:45P
    The same, with a 12-hour time.

Notes
  The separators, and whether the time is shown with 12 or 24 hours,
  follow COUNTRY= in CONFIG.SYS. DOS sets the time of every file it writes
  from this clock. On a PC/AT or later, TIME also sets the battery-backed
  clock.
