Topic: MEM
Summary: Shows the memory in use and free
Origin: MS-DOS 4.00
Group: command
Files: MEM.EXE

Syntax
  MEM [/PROGRAM | /DEBUG] [/HUMAN]

  /PROGRAM     Lists what is in memory, block by block, with its address,
               name, size and type: the parts of DOS, each program and its
               environment, and the free blocks.
  /DEBUG       As /PROGRAM, and lists the device drivers too, and the
               EMS handles when there is expanded memory.
  /HUMAN       Shows the totals in KiB, MiB or GiB, as DF -H does,
               rather than in bytes.

Examples
  MEM
    Shows the total and free conventional memory, and the largest
    program that can be run.
  MEM /PROGRAM | MORE
    Lists the programs in memory, a screen at a time.
  MEM /HUMAN
    Shows the totals as 640.0KiB, 575.4KiB and so on.

Notes
  Sizes are in bytes, and the addresses and sizes of the lists in hex.
  MEM also shows the total and free expanded (EMS) memory, when an EMS
  driver is loaded, and the extended memory of an AT or later. Only one
  of /PROGRAM and /DEBUG may be given.

  With /HUMAN, a total below 1 KiB is in bytes (512B); a larger one has
  one decimal, rounded up, in KiB, MiB or GiB (1 KiB is 1024 bytes),
  so 589,136 bytes is 575.4KiB. /HUMAN is a GR-DOS addition; the lists
  stay in hex. In GR-DOS, MEM is compiled with Open Watcom C rather than
  Microsoft C; without /HUMAN its output is the same.
