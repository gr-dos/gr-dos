Topic: VERIFY
Summary: Sets whether DOS checks what it writes to disk
Origin: MS-DOS 4.00
Group: internal

Syntax
  VERIFY [ON | OFF]

  ON           Has the disk driver read back each write to check that it
               can be read.
  OFF          Writes without checking. The default.

Examples
  VERIFY
    Shows whether VERIFY is on or off.
  VERIFY ON
    Checks every write from now on, for all programs.

Notes
  VERIFY ON slows writing, and checks only that the data can be read, not
  that it matches the original. COPY /V and XCOPY /V turn it on for just
  that copy.
