Topic: SHIFT
Summary: Moves a batch file's parameters down by one
Origin: MS-DOS 4.00
Group: internal

Syntax
  SHIFT

Examples
  SHIFT
    Makes %2 the new %1, %3 the new %2, and so on; the tenth
    parameter, if any, becomes %9.

Notes
  A batch file sees only %0 through %9 at a time. SHIFT lets it work
  through more than nine parameters, or through any number in a loop:

    :NEXT
    IF "%1"=="" GOTO END
    COPY %1 A:
    SHIFT
    GOTO NEXT
    :END

  There is no way to shift back. SHIFT does nothing at the prompt.
