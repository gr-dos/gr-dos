Topic: SORT
Summary: Sorts lines of text
Origin: MS-DOS 4.00
Group: command
Files: SORT.EXE

Syntax
  SORT [/R] [/+n] [< input] [> output]
  command | SORT [/R] [/+n]

  /R           Sorts in reverse order, Z to A and 9 to 0.
  /+n          Sorts on the text from column n of each line (1 if left
               out).

  SORT reads its standard input, given with < or a pipe (|), and writes
  the sorted lines to its standard output.

Examples
  SORT < NAMES.TXT > SORTED.TXT
    Writes the lines of NAMES.TXT, in order, to SORTED.TXT.
  DIR | SORT
    Lists the directory in order of name.
  SORT /R < SCORES.TXT
    Shows SCORES.TXT in reverse order.

Notes
  SORT orders the lines by the country's collating sequence, in which
  upper and lower case letters sort together. Numbers are sorted as
  text: 10 comes before 9. The input can be at most 64 KB.
