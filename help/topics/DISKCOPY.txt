Topic: DISKCOPY
Summary: Copies a whole floppy disk to another of the same format
Origin: MS-DOS 4.00
Group: command
Files: DISKCOPY.COM

Syntax
  DISKCOPY [drive1: [drive2:]] [/1]

  drive1:      The floppy drive holding the disk to copy (the source).
  drive2:      The drive holding the disk to copy to (the target). It may
               be the same drive: DISKCOPY then asks you to swap disks.
  /1           Copies only the first side of the disk.

Examples
  DISKCOPY A: B:
    Copies the disk in A: to the disk in B:.
  DISKCOPY A: A:
    Copies with one drive, asking for the source and target in turn.

Exit status
  0  The copy ran (see its messages for read or write errors).
  1  A bad parameter, or drives or disks that cannot be used.
  2  Stopped with Ctrl+C.

Notes
  DISKCOPY copies track by track, so the target becomes an exact copy:
  everything that was on it is lost. Write-protect the source disk so
  that swapping the disks by mistake cannot harm it. Both drives must
  take the same format; a target that is not formatted is formatted
  while copying. The copy gets its own serial number. DISKCOPY works on
  floppies only, not on a hard disk, a SUBSTed, ASSIGNed or JOINed
  drive, or a network drive. Check a copy with DISKCOMP.
