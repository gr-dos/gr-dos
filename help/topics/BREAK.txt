Topic: BREAK
Summary: Sets how often DOS checks for Ctrl+C
Origin: MS-DOS 4.00
Group: internal

Syntax
  BREAK [ON | OFF]

  ON           Checks for Ctrl+C (and Ctrl+Break) at every DOS call, such
               as each disk read or write.
  OFF          Checks only when a program reads the keyboard or writes to
               the screen, printer or a serial port. The default.

Examples
  BREAK
    Shows whether BREAK is on or off.
  BREAK ON
    Lets Ctrl+C stop a program that is busy with the disk.

Notes
  BREAK ON makes a long compile or sort easier to stop, at a small cost in
  speed. The setting can also be made when the computer starts, with
  BREAK=ON in CONFIG.SYS.
