Topic: DISKCOMP
Summary: Compares two floppy disks track by track
Origin: MS-DOS 4.00
Group: command
Files: DISKCOMP.COM

Syntax
  DISKCOMP [drive1: [drive2:]] [/1] [/8]

  drive1:      The drive holding the first disk.
  drive2:      The drive holding the second disk. It may be the same
               drive: DISKCOMP then asks you to swap disks.
  /1           Compares only the first side of the disks.
  /8           Compares only the first 8 sectors of each track.

Examples
  DISKCOMP A: B:
    Compares the disk in A: with the disk in B:.

Notes
  DISKCOMP reads both disks and reports each track that differs, by side
  and track number, or "Compare OK" when they are the same. It is meant
  to check a DISKCOPY: two disks with the same files copied by COPY or
  XCOPY may still differ. Both disks must be of the same format, and the
  drives must be floppy drives. Its exit status is 0 even when the disks
  differ: 1 means a bad parameter or drive, 2 Ctrl+C.
