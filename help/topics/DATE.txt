Topic: DATE
Summary: Shows or sets the date
Origin: MS-DOS 4.00
Group: internal

Syntax
  DATE [date]

  date         The new date, in the country's order: mm-dd-yy in the US,
               dd-mm-yy or yy-mm-dd elsewhere. - / or . may separate the
               parts, and the year may have two or four digits.

Examples
  DATE
    Shows the date and asks for a new one; press Enter to keep it.
  DATE 3-15-1989
    Sets the date to March 15, 1989 (in the US order).

Notes
  The order follows COUNTRY= in CONFIG.SYS. Years from 1980 through 2099
  may be set. DOS sets the date of every file it writes from this date.
  On a PC/AT or later, DATE also sets the battery-backed clock.
