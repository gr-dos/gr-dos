Topic: TICO
Summary: A full-screen text editor
Origin: tico16
Group: command
Files: TICO.EXE

Syntax
  TICO [[drive:][path]filename]

  filename     The text file to edit.

Examples
  TICO NOTES.TXT
    Edits the file NOTES.TXT.
  TICO A:\AUTOEXEC.BAT
    Edits the AUTOEXEC.BAT of the floppy in A:.

Notes
  TICO is the tico16 editor, version 0.04, from
  github.com/tico-edit/tico16, under the MIT license. It is not built
  from source here: GR-DOS ships the release's TICO.EXE, checked against
  a pinned checksum. Its keys are shown in its own help. TICO is on every
  GR-DOS floppy, and takes the place of EDLIN on the 360 KB one.
