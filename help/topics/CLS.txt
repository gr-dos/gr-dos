Topic: CLS
Summary: Clears the screen
Origin: MS-DOS 4.00
Group: internal

Syntax
  CLS

Examples
  CLS
    Clears the screen and puts the prompt at the top.

Notes
  CLS uses the screen's current colors and size. With ANSI.SYS loaded, or
  when its output is redirected, it sends the ANSI clear-screen sequence
  instead of using the BIOS.
